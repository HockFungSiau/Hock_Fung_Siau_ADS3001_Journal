## Week 10: Project Status

**Status:** Backend and front end are complete. All that is left is the testing stage, which is limited because the Data Vault is still missing data.

## Since my last update

In Week 6, the backend was around 60% finished, and around then I doubted whether I would finish. Now the whole backend is built and connected to a fully working front end. On top of this, I added extra parts to the build, including the suggestions from my presentation (Week 7), that further improve how the calculations and analysis are done. The front end was my biggest worry at the start, as I had never built one, but looking at what I have done now, I feel quite a bit of satisfaction and am proud of what I have developed.

## What is working

The dashboard can now extract data, create the necessary calculations and flag any risk that may occur. My manual layer from Week 5 also shows what data is missing. While doing this, I compared some of the totals to the team’s actual calculations (Week 9), and the ones I could compare all matched.

## Known issues

The largest and only issue is that the Data Vault is still missing a portion of the data, so I can’t say the whole dashboard is correct. The request to fill it in is still open.

## What this week taught me

This week I spent more time just creating a good product, which gave me room to reflect on what I have done and the worries I had coming into this project. In Week 1, my biggest fear was letting the team down. Now I feel I have met the goal they gave me, which was to create a working dashboard. In Week 6, my doubt about finishing was about something I controlled, and I got there. What I do not control is how VFMC manages its data. Because “done” means built and tested, and I have only done the first, I cannot fully say that I am done. I am no longer asking whether I will finish the project, but whether the team can trust it.
