# Week 8: Due diligence report on my own dashboard

## Subject: The Manager Concentration Dashboard

**Strengths**: Overall, it works end-to-end, and the presentation last week went well. The suggestions made in my presentation were added, and my manual layer now also shows what’s missing, so the gaps can be fixed by people with higher authority.

**Red flags**: There is a lot of missing data, so I could only test the parts that have data and cannot say the whole dashboard is correct. The danger is that it looks finished. It may look strong on the surface, but what sits underneath may be wrong.

**Questions for management**: Who can create the fixes? Last week showed me that I did not know who owned what, and I still do not this week. I cannot fill the data myself, and I could not have built any of this without the data team.

**Recommendation**: Proceed with conditions: promising, but unverified until the data is filled. Last week, Ben suggested I look to the data team to fill in the data. But that is a fairly large team with different people doing different things, and on top of that, many different departments work on different parts of the Data Vault. This week I found it hard to find the correct person to help with this part of the project, so at the end of the week I passed the details on. I felt understanding rather than relief.

**What I have been taught this week**: The most important lesson this week is that a business works best when people know their roles in the workplace and where everyone else sits and works together. I felt that if I knew who to look for, I would be able to progress this data-filling part faster.
