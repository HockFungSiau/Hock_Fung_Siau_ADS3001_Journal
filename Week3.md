# Week 3: How to get to real workplace data

In university, data arrives easily as files, but at VFMC, all the data lives in their Data Vault, which is an internal platform. During this week, it turned out that getting this data would be a small hassle and took a while. But honestly understanding a good work culture, it would have been quite fast. My experience and lessons could be applied to most tasks done in the workplace. Here’s what I would tell someone starting on how to get access to the VFMC data vault I did:

## 1) Accept that access is part of the project, not a step.
On your first day, you would not simply just get access. There are layers of permissions and safety mechanisms around it. The first thing to do is to raise a ticket immediately, but nothing can start until it gets cleared

## 2) Don’t just sit on the ticket
It took a while before my ticket was answered, which was quite frustrating, but understandable with the many people who work at VFMC. I found that asking people in person is much better than just sending a message. I found out who owns the platform, spoke to them directly, and got access. The fastest route to a process here is always going to be a person-to-person interaction.

## 3) Talk to the owner about the system
Speaking to the person who maintains the vault, I understood why the controls exist. My frustration gently disappeared. It is a small thing, but I’ve noted that understanding why something is difficult makes it much less annoying to work with and in a matter of fact, you can find and utilise the benefits of their system.

## 4) Find someone who knows the data
Meeting up with others who have more experience can speed up your understanding. I personally met up with VFMC’s data analyst, Mimi. She helped me find the things I was looking for in the dataset, narrowing hundreds of different data points to the 20 I would need.  What I found is that the team is all connected. Everyone is connected in some way, so being introduced to someone never feels awkward

## Overall
I found that the fastest way to finish the task was getting to know the people you work with. VFMC has 187 people working as a team, not as individuals. Overall, I learnt this week how a company operates together as one and not individuals, and through working efficiently with others I was able to speed up my process to start building my dashboard.
