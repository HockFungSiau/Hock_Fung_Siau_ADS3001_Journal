# Week 12: Message to my Week 1 self

Dear Week 1 me,

You are going to be nervous walking into your first office job. You will have no finance knowledge, you will have never built a working dashboard, and your biggest fear will be letting your team down. You will also believe that work is a rigid, structured day, but this is not true. Here is what actually happens.

The hard part isn’t the code. It is understanding what the numbers mean and who owns them. This is real-world data now, so there will be blanks, but that has been taught to you many times during your studies. What you will really be taught is to know where everyone sits and how the teams work together, so you know who to go to. It is essential that you stop hiding in technical detail when explaining things to people and stop having negative thoughts when asking for help.

On day one, when you need something done or need to get started, James will tell you that 50% of the job is being likeable. Do not take this lightly. When you get to know the people around you, you will find that they are more willing to talk and help out if you are likeable. Take the odd morning coffee with the team and you will see how bonding is how people end up being more willing to help each other out.

Towards the very end of the project, do not worry about not being able to finish. This is your first time creating a proper working dashboard, and the final logic and ideas will bog you down a lot. Just ensure that a good effort is put in and that many questions are asked, whether they are simple or complex. Create a good plan and always prepare for the worst, as many unexpected things may happen. This is a real-world project, and no plan will ever have a linear finish. Testing won’t be fully finished because of the missing data, so be honest with James early and leave clear handover documents.

Note that not all meetings and things done will be very traditional. You may end up having meetings in cafés or even at a park, rather than in a conference room. James said it himself: not every meeting needs to be in an office. Work will be less rigid than you expected, and relaxing and working fit together.

As for whether this experience will make you better in your next job, there is no certainty. Data science is an ever-growing industry, and your technical side will forever grow. But what this internship will teach you is the business side and creating connections with your team. Those human connections and teamwork will never change.

Kind regards,

Me, Week 12
